IN

First Step

Verified against IHCDA directly (call, 7/31/26) + Feb 2026 Universal Program Guide

Purpose: Help first-time homebuyers cover the down payment — often the biggest barrier to purchasing.

Key features

  • Available to first-time homebuyers, qualified veterans, or buyers purchasing in an eligible targeted census tract or whole targeted county.
  • Provides 5% down payment assistance based on purchase price.
  • Non-forgivable second mortgage, no monthly payment. Balance due when the first mortgage terminates, property stops being primary residence, is sold, refinanced (outside an IHCDA refinance program), or a HELOC is taken.
  • 30-year fixed-rate FHA or Conventional financing through participating lenders.

Ideal borrower

Has the income to qualify for a mortgage but hasn't saved enough for the down payment and closing costs.
Source: in.gov/ihcda/homebuyers
IN

Step Down

Interest rate discount — no DPA
Verified against IHCDA directly (call, 7/31/26)

Purpose: Reduce the monthly mortgage payment instead of providing cash assistance.

Key features

  • Available to eligible first-time homebuyers, qualified veterans, or buyers in a targeted tract/county — same eligibility structure as First Step.
  • Below-market interest rate on the first mortgage.
  • No down payment assistance included.
  • 30-year fixed-rate FHA or Conventional financing.

Ideal borrower

Already has funds for the purchase, but wants to maximize long-term affordability through a lower monthly payment.
Source: in.gov/ihcda/homebuyers
IN

Next Home

Verified against IHCDA directly (call, 7/31/26)

Purpose: Extend IHCDA down payment assistance to a broader range of buyers.

Key features

  • Does not require first-time-buyer status — available to first-time AND repeat buyers.
  • 3.5% down payment assistance as a non-forgivable second mortgage.
  • Balance due when the first mortgage terminates, property stops being primary residence, is sold, refinanced (outside an IHCDA refinance program), or a HELOC is taken.
  • Subject to the same income/acquisition limits as First Step.

Ideal borrower

Purchasing another home, relocating, or otherwise ineligible for First Step, but still wants help with upfront costs.
Source: in.gov/ihcda/homebuyers
IN

Next Step

Refinance only
Verified against IHCDA directly (call, 7/31/26)

Purpose: Help existing IHCDA borrowers refinance their mortgage.

Key features

  • One-time refinance opportunity for homeowners with an eligible First Place (retired program) or First Step mortgage.
  • 30-year fixed-rate FHA or Conventional refinancing.
  • Non-forgivable DPA carried over where applicable under the refinance.
  • Not a purchase-assistance program — not shown to new buyers in the app.

Ideal borrower

An existing IHCDA homeowner whose mortgage qualifies and who could benefit from refinancing.
Source: in.gov/ihcda/homebuyers

Florida Hometown Heroes

Verified against Florida Housing Finance Corporation, 7/31/26

Purpose: Help essential-workforce and first-time buyers cover down payment and closing costs.

Key features

  • $10,000–$35,000 as a 0% interest deferred second mortgage.
  • Eligibility gates on employer location (must be FL-based) and occupation, not the applicant's current state of residence — an out-of-state buyer relocating for a qualifying FL job can still qualify.
  • Veterans are exempt from the occupation, employer, and first-time-buyer requirements.
  • Also offers a lower first-mortgage rate as part of the program.

Ideal borrower

Florida essential-workforce employee (teacher, healthcare worker, first responder, etc.) or a veteran, buying a primary residence in FL.
Source: floridahousing.org

Miami-Dade County Homebuyer DPA

Verified against Miami-Dade County directly, 7/31/26 — income limits corrected this session

Purpose: Help first-time buyers currently residing in Miami-Dade County cover their down payment.

Key features

  • Up to $35,000 as an interest-free deferred loan, up to 30 years, no monthly payment.
  • Must be a Miami-Dade County resident at time of application and loan closing — unlike Hometown Heroes, this one really does gate on current residency.
  • Requires HUD-approved homebuyer counseling and education.
  • Income limits by household size: 1 — $95,620 · 2 — $109,200 · 3 — $122,920 · 4 — $136,500. (5+ not yet verified.)
  • Shared-equity clawback on resale: 100% of profit due to the County if sold within 1–3 years, 50% within 4–6 years, 0% after 6 years.

Ideal borrower

First-time buyer already living in Miami-Dade County who plans to stay in the home long-term (the resale clawback matters most for short holds).
Contact: Shawn Topps, (786) 469-2209 — for anything beyond household size 4.
Source: miamidade.gov

Florida Assist (FL Assist)

Verified against Florida Housing Standard Bond Guide 6.25.26 (Lakeview), effective 05/06/2026 — income & purchase price limits populated and live in the app this session (7/31/26)

Purpose: Down payment and closing cost assistance paired with a Florida Housing first mortgage (Standard Bond/Standard TBA).

Key features

  • $10,000, 0% interest, non-amortizing, deferred second mortgage. No monthly payment.
  • Not forgivable — due in full upon sale, transfer, satisfaction/refinance of the first mortgage, or when the borrower stops occupying the home as their primary residence.
  • Available with FL First (Gov't FHA/USDA-RD/VA), HFA Preferred (Fannie Mae), or HFA Advantage (Freddie Mac) first mortgages.
  • Mutually exclusive with FL HLP and HFA Preferred/Advantage PLUS — a buyer picks exactly one. Not enforced by the matching engine yet; all three can currently show as simultaneous "matches."
  • County-level income limits (NonTargeted) verified and live: 1–2 person and 3+ person tiers.

Ideal borrower

FL first-time buyer using a Standard Bond/TBA first mortgage, needs modest down payment help, comfortably under the county's non-targeted income limit.
Updated 8/8/26: the earlier bug (a hardcoded reference to IHCDA on FL results) is fixed — the matching engine now resolves the correct agency name per state. targeted_lookup_table is live again for this program. What's still open: real census-tract matching isn't built yet, so a buyer in one of Florida Housing's targeted counties currently gets a "pending verification" caveat correctly naming Florida Housing, rather than a computed pass/fail against the (higher) targeted-area figures. Same permanent-caveat design as IHCDA's own targeted-tract handling until real tract matching is built.
Source: Florida Housing Standard Bond Guide 6.25.26 (Lakeview) — PDF provided directly; general org site floridahousing.org

Florida Homeownership Loan Program (FL HLP)

Verified against Florida Housing Standard Bond Guide 6.25.26 (Lakeview), effective 05/06/2026 — income & purchase price limits populated and live in the app this session (7/31/26)

Purpose: Down payment and closing cost assistance paired with a Florida Housing first mortgage, for buyers who can absorb a small second-mortgage payment.

Key features

  • $12,500, 3% interest, fully-amortizing second mortgage, 30-year term. Carries a real monthly payment (~$52.70/mo at these terms) — factor into DTI.
  • Not forgivable — balance due in full upon sale, transfer, satisfaction/refinance of the first mortgage, or when the borrower stops occupying the home.
  • Same eligible first-mortgage pairings as FL Assist.
  • Mutually exclusive with FL Assist and HFA Preferred/Advantage PLUS — same not-yet-enforced caveat as FL Assist.
  • Real finding this session: HLP's NonTargeted 3+ Person income figures are not identical to FL Assist's in 9 counties — Baker, Collier, Gulf, Martin, Monroe, Okaloosa, Polk, St. Lucie, Walton — confirmed by direct row-by-row comparison of both tables in the source guide. Kept as fully separate lookup tables for this reason; do not assume they're interchangeable.

Ideal borrower

FL first-time buyer who wants a slightly larger DPA amount than FL Assist and can handle a small added monthly payment within their DTI.
Updated 8/8/26: same as FL Assist — the IHCDA-hardcoding bug is fixed and targeted_lookup_table is live again. Real census-tract matching still not built; targeted-county buyers get a "pending verification" caveat correctly naming Florida Housing rather than a computed pass/fail.
Source: Florida Housing Standard Bond Guide 6.25.26 (Lakeview) — PDF provided directly; general org site floridahousing.org

HFA Preferred/Advantage PLUS Second Mortgage

PLUS TBA only
Verified against Florida Housing Standard Bond Guide 6.25.26 (Lakeview) for terms — income/purchase price limits still not populated as of 7/31/26

Purpose: Forgivable down payment assistance for buyers using specific conventional (non-government) first mortgages.

Key features

  • 3%, 4%, or 5% of the total first mortgage loan amount as a forgivable second mortgage. Forgiven 20%/year over 5 years. 0% interest, no monthly payment.
  • Only available paired with HFA Preferred (Fannie Mae) or HFA Advantage (Freddie Mac) conventional first mortgages — not FHA/VA/USDA.
  • Confirmed to be a PLUS TBA-only product — there is no "PLUS BOND" offering on Florida Housing's rate sheet, so this doesn't pair with the Standard Bond program at all.
  • Not stackable with FL Assist or FL HLP.

Ideal borrower

FL first-time buyer using HFA Preferred or HFA Advantage conventional financing who wants the assistance forgiven over time rather than repaid.
Still shows "pending verification" in the live app — this Bond Guide has no income/purchase price limit table for PLUS at all (it only covers FL Assist and FL HLP). Needs the separate PLUS TBA / Standard TBA lender guide as its own source before it can be populated — a different document than the one used for FL Assist/HLP.
Source: Florida Housing Standard Bond Guide 6.25.26 (Lakeview) for product terms; eHousingPlus rate page for PLUS TBA confirmation

AL

AHFA Step Up

Verified against AHFA directly, 7/31/26 — data corrected this session (fabricated income tier removed)

Purpose: Statewide down payment assistance for Alabama homebuyers.

Key features

  • 4% of sales price, up to $10,000, as a 10-year second mortgage.
  • No first-time-buyer requirement.
  • Flat income limit: $172,800, regardless of household size or location — no tiering.
  • Minimum credit score 640, DTI ≤ 45%.

Ideal borrower

Any Alabama buyer (first-time or repeat) who needs help with the down payment and fits within the flat income cap.
Source: ahfa.com

UK Employer Assisted Housing Program (EAHP)

Verified against UK HR directly (Azetta Beatty, 7/31/26)

Purpose: Homebuying incentive for University of Kentucky faculty/staff buying near campus.

Key features

  • Forgivable loan: 5% of purchase price, up to $15,000. Forgiven 20% per year over 5 years.
  • Employer must be University of Kentucky or UK HealthCare.
  • Faculty: only instructor or assistant professor rank is eligible, and immediately (no tenure/waiting period).
  • Staff: eligible by maximum grade — ≤46 general staff, ≤10 hospital staff — after a 90-day tenure period (faculty are exempt from this wait).
  • Must purchase in a designated neighborhood near campus.

Ideal borrower

UK instructor/assistant professor, or staff at or below the qualifying grade, buying near campus.
Designated-zone boundary is a 2013 scanned map — no digital/GIS version exists (confirmed directly with UK HR, 7/31/26). Always verify a specific address manually against the map; this is a permanent limitation, not a pending fix.
Source: UK HR Benefits — direct correspondence, 7/31/26
